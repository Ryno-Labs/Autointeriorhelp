How to choose the right product

Buy for the surface and problem, not for the loudest label.

1

Know the surface

Leather, cloth, carpet, vinyl and plastic need different chemistry.

2

Start mild

Use the least aggressive method that can solve the problem.

3

Check compatibility

Test in a hidden area before working on a visible surface.

4

Value the outcome

The cheapest product is not always the lowest-cost solution.
